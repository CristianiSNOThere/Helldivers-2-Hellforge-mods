# Sickle Laser AR v0.1.2

Both Sickles have a base fire rate of 700 RPM, zero horizontal and vertical spread, and 2,000 m/s projectiles at every heat tier with no distance damage fall-off.

| Weapon / heat tier | Normal / durable damage | Armor penetration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LAS-16 Sickle | 70 / 10 | AP3 |
| LAS-17 Double-Edge: cold | 65 / 10 | AP3 |
| LAS-17 Double-Edge: warm | 65 / 10 | AP3 |
| LAS-17 Double-Edge: hot | 83 / 14 | AP3 |
| LAS-17 Double-Edge: maximum heat | 83 / 14 | AP4 |

LAS-16 generates 50% less heat per shot, from 1.15 to 0.575. Double-Edge's direct heat self-damage is reduced by 25%. Double-Edge heat buildup, cooling and maximum-heat ignition remain unchanged. General fire damage remains unchanged.

## Install

Import the ZIP into Arsenal, enable its option, keep Bingus Shared Loader at its documented priority, Purge and Deploy, then restart Helldivers 2. Allow up to two minutes after reaching the ship for initialization. Requires Helldivers 2 Steam build 25480438 and Bingus Shared Loader v19.

Use the individual packages or the complete modpack. Enabling both duplicates the same add-on resources.
