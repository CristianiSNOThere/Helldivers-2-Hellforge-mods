# VG-70 Variable Portable Maxigun Rework v0.1.0

The primary-slot VG-70 Variable keeps its mobile, backpack-free reload and gains a 150-round magazine. Auto offers 550, 750 and 1,000 RPM, with normal/durable damage of 110/29, 90/24 and 75/20 per bullet respectively; AP3. A new selection starts at 1,000 RPM, and native selections are remembered across modes.

Volley fires 15 simultaneous bullets at 40 normal / 11 durable damage each, AP3. Volley follows the selected 550, 750 or 1,000 RPM rate, keeping the same damage at every rate. Total fires all rounds remaining in the magazine at 28 normal / 8 durable damage per bullet, AP4, with spread reduced to 0.75 on both axes. A full 150-round Total shot has 4,200 normal / 1,200 durable raw damage before armor and hit-location effects.

The weapon retains 6 starting spare magazines, up to 7 maximum, and receives 7 per resupply. Base ergonomics increases from 25 to 40, and sway decreases from 1.0 to 0.9. Recoil decreases 15% in every mode: horizontal/vertical drift becomes 4.25/8.5 from 5/10, and climb becomes 3.4/6.8 from 4/8. Total retains its stock right-arm self-injury and knockdown behavior. Sustained Auto muzzle flashes stay aligned. Reload timing and animation, projectile speed and sounds are retained.

Requires Helldivers 2 Steam build 25480438 and Bingus Shared Loader v19. Import the ZIP into Arsenal, enable its option, Purge and Deploy, then restart. Use this individual ZIP or a compatible Super Earth Arsenal Mod Pack, not both.
