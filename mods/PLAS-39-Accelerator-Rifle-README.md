# PLAS-39 Accelerator Rifle v1.1.10

Magazine capacity is 21 rounds. Horizontal and vertical recoil drift and camera recoil are 2, horizontal and vertical spread are 0.3, sway is 0.4, and stagger is 25. Direct-hit and blast armor penetration are 4, and outer blast and shockwave radii are 2.5 m. Semi fires one round with a 0.01-second minimum charge at a base rate of 200 RPM (0.30-second spacing). Burst fires three rounds, consumes three rounds, charges for at least 0.45 seconds and spaces its rounds 0.12 seconds apart. Both modes use normal firing audio.

## Changes in v1.1.10

Fixes PLAS-39 Semi/Burst switching and firing audio when the game applies its fire-rate modifier. Both modes retain that modifier and follow the selected Weapon Functions mode.

## Controls

Hold R and use Weapon Wheel Left (default: left-click) in the left side of Weapon Functions to select Semi or Burst. Custom bindings use the native selector. The selected mode is remembered, and each active rifle follows its own selection. The game's fire-rate modifier can increase Semi's shot spacing from 0.30 to approximately 0.333 seconds; the three-round Burst spacing remains 0.12 seconds.

## Install

Requires Helldivers 2 Steam build 25480438 and Bingus Shared Loader v19. Import the ZIP into Arsenal, enable its option, Purge and Deploy, then restart Helldivers 2. Choose individual mods or the combined modpack to avoid duplicate resources.
