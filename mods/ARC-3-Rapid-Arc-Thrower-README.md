# ARC-3 Rapid/Supercharge v0.14

ARC-3 offers Auto/Rapid and Semi/Supercharge. Rapid charges in 0.307692/0.338462/0.369231 seconds and deals approximately 192.1 normal / 76.5 durable damage. Supercharge charges in 3.4/3.5/3.75 seconds, deals 2325 normal / 2325 durable damage, targets one enemy, and cannot explode from overcharge. Both modes have AP7, 45 m reach and stock ARC audio.

## Changes in v0.14

Supercharge charge time falls from 4.9/5.0/5.25 to 3.4/3.5/3.75 seconds. Normal and durable damage increase from 1550/1550 to 2325/2325, a 50% increase. Armor penetration remains AP7 in both modes.

## Fire modes

Hold R and choose Auto for Rapid or Semi for Supercharge. Rapid uses a 0.85 damage multiplier on its 226/90 base pulse, with Stun Medium buildup 1.2, demolition 4, force 25, impulse 2 and camera climb 0.4/0.4. Supercharge uses a 1.0 damage multiplier, Stun Medium buildup 12 and Stun Massive buildup 25, with chain 1 and split 0.

## Install

Requires Helldivers 2 Steam build 25480438 and Bingus Shared Loader v19. Import the ZIP into Arsenal, enable its option, Purge and Deploy, then restart. Use individual packages or the complete modpack. When using Gas Overhaul, update it to v0.15 alongside this ARC update. Enable Arc Thrower Revamped in CowboyBingus's Vanilla Plus Megapack for hold-to-fire.
