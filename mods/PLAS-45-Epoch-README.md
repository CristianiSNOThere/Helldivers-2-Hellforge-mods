# PLAS-45 Epoch Highest-Charge Rework v1.0.0

PLAS-45 Epoch highest-charge shots gain 30% more normal and durable direct-hit and blast damage, burning on blast hits, a 5 m full-damage radius, 6 m outer damage radius and 7 m shockwave radius. Magazine capacity increases from 3 to 4 rounds and maximum spare magazines from 4 to 5. Reload while walking with the existing reload animation.

## Highest-charge shot

The changes apply to the 2.5/2.6-second highest-charge tier. Base direct damage increases from 400 normal / 200 durable to 520 normal / 260 durable; base explosion damage increases from 800 normal / 800 durable to 1040 normal / 1040 durable. Both increases are 30%, before the existing charge multiplier, armor, hit location and blast falloff. Full-damage/outer-damage/shockwave radii increase from 3/4/5 m to 5/6/7 m. Enemies struck by the damaging blast receive standard burning; the shot creates no persistent ground-fire pool.

Lower-charge shots retain their existing damage, blast and armor penetration. Charge times remain 1.0/2.5/2.6 seconds, and the existing 1.0/2.0 charge damage multipliers remain. The highest-charge shot retains AP5.

## Magazine and reload

The whole weapon receives a 4-round magazine instead of 3 and a maximum of 5 spare magazines instead of 4. It still starts with 2 spare magazines and receives 2 per resupply. Reload while walking with the same Epoch reload clips and timing.

## Install

Requires Helldivers 2 Steam build 25480438 and Bingus Shared Loader v19. Import the ZIP into Arsenal, enable its option, Purge and Deploy, then restart. Allow initialization to finish before calling in Epoch. Use individual mods or the combined modpack. If installing the individual AC-8 mod alongside Epoch, update AC-8 to v1.0.2 as well.
