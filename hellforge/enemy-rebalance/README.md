# Enemy Rebalance v0.7.0

[Download v0.7.0](https://github.com/CristianiSNOThere/Helldivers-2-Hellforge-mods/releases/tag/hellforge-enemy-rebalance-v0.7.0) | [Installation](INSTALL.md)

## Bile Titan

Regular Bile Titans take full explosion damage: their explosion damage multiplier increases from 0.5 to 1.0.

## Impaler

Head health decreases from 1,250 to 900. Each of the four leg-flesh zones decreases from 1,000 to 500 health. Destroying one of these fatal zones kills the Impaler.

## Alpha Warrior

Movement speed decreases by 20%. Guard, patrol and investigate speeds decrease from 2.5 to 2.0; combat and strafe speeds decrease from 6.0 to 4.8.

## Alpha Commander

The cooldown between Alpha Warrior summons increases from 15–20 seconds to 35–45 seconds.

## Overseers

Elevated Overseer armor health decreases by 25%, rounded to the nearest whole point: 100 becomes 75, 150 becomes 113, and 200 becomes 150. Crescent Overseer armor health decreases by 50%: 100 becomes 50, 150 becomes 75, and 200 becomes 100.

## Charger

Charger braking uses the updated rear-lift animation. The recovery pause with its rear raised lasts an additional 0.5 seconds, with the same charge and initial braking speed.

## Automaton accuracy

Eligible projectile weapons have 25% wider horizontal and vertical spread. This covers Troopers and Commissars, Devastators, Hulks, Gunships, Scout and Reinforced Striders, War Striders, and fixed bot guns including command bunker weapons. Tanks, Factory Striders and Cyborgs retain their existing spread.

## Requirements

Requires HellForge SDK 13 or newer and HellForge game build 25480438. Prepare the Charger animation locally from an updated Helldivers 2 installation using [INSTALL.md](INSTALL.md).
